Features

From recording to decisions, without sending anything over the Internet.

Recording

Capture any meeting

  • Online meetings: Teams, Zoom, Meet, webinars
  • In-room meetings, with the microphone
  • Both at once: your voice and the other participants'
  • Import audio and video files (MP3, M4A, WAV, MP4, MKV, MOV…)
  • Automatic switch when you plug in a headset
  • “Test sound” button before an important meeting
Teams, Zoom, MeetIn-room meetingAudio or video file

Transcription

Accurate text, no graphics card

  • Timestamped text, easy to read and quote
  • English, French or automatic detection
  • Several accuracy levels, and a fast mode for recordings lasting several hours
  • Automatic resume after an interruption
  • Works offline

Trimming

A recording left running

  • An overview shows where people are speaking
  • Start and end chosen to the minute, or as durations
  • Listen to each boundary before deciding
  • Transcribe only that part, or cut the recording
  • Optional: stop when nobody is speaking, stop after a maximum duration, discreet reminder, icon near the clock

Keep 2:05 pm → 3:20 pm

Summaries

The essentials, in one click

  • Summary, key points and action items
  • Optional context: participants, roles, topic
  • Long meetings supported
  • Written in the interface language
  • Choice of AI models, with Ollama

Discussion

Ask the meeting your questions

  • “Who approved the budget?”, “What are the deadlines?”
  • Answers based only on the meeting
  • Follow-up questions that remember the conversation
  • Offline dictation: ask your questions out loud

History and exports

Find everything, share easily

  • Search across all transcripts
  • Export to Markdown, Excel, text and subtitles
  • Full database, for Power BI, PostgreSQL or Python
  • Optional backup to your Google Drive or OneDrive, one folder per computer

Features

Everything to make the most of your meetings

From recording to decisions: TranscriPilote covers every step, without sending anything over the internet.

Recording

Capture any meeting

Record straight from TranscriPilote, whether the meeting is online or in a room.

  • Online meetings: Teams, Zoom, Meet, webinars…
  • Microphone for in-person meetings
  • Import audio and video files (MP3, M4A, WAV, MP4, MKV, MOV…)
  • Record while another meeting is being processed
Transcription

Faithful text, no GPU needed

A state-of-the-art speech recognition engine runs directly on your processor.

  • Timestamped text, easy to review and quote
  • French, English or automatic detection
  • Several accuracy levels, from fastest to finest
  • Works offline
Speakers

Who said what? Beta

TranscriPilote recognizes the different voices and attributes each sentence to its speaker.

  • Speakers identified and colored in the transcription
  • Number of participants automatic or chosen
  • Beta feature, still in development: turn it on when useful
Summaries

The essentials, in one click

An artificial intelligence installed on your PC reads the transcription and draws out the essentials.

  • Overall summary, key points and action items
  • Add context (participants, topic) for a more accurate result
  • Long meetings supported
  • A choice of AI models, excellent in French and English
Discussion

Ask the meeting your questions

A dedicated discussion space to question a transcription as you would a colleague.

  • “Who approved the budget?”, “What are the deadlines?”
  • Answers based only on what the meeting contains
  • Follow-up questions that take the conversation into account
History and backup

Find everything, share easily

Every meeting is kept in a local database, available at any time.

  • History with search across transcriptions
  • Markdown export of summaries and reports, Excel, text and subtitles
  • Complete downloadable database, for Power BI, PostgreSQL or Python
  • Optional backup to your Google Drive or OneDrive
  • Background tasks, cancellable at any time

Free, no sign-up, installed in a few minutes.

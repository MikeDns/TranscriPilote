Privacy policy

What TranscriPilote and this website do with your data, and what they don't.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

In short

TranscriPilote processes your meetings on your computer. Neither the publisher nor anyone else has access to your recordings, transcripts or summaries. The app contains no analytics, no trackers and no ads.

The app

Your recordings, transcripts, summaries and settings are kept in the %LOCALAPPDATA%\TranscriPilote folder of your Windows account. They are not sent anywhere.

The app connects to the Internet only in the following cases:

  • checking for and downloading new versions, on GitHub (can be turned off in the settings);
  • downloading the transcription models, once, on first use, from Hugging Face;
  • downloading the FFmpeg video conversion tool, once, if you import a video;
  • copying your data to your own Google Drive or OneDrive, if you turn on backup: it goes straight from your PC to your account;
  • using speaker identification (beta), which downloads a model with the Hugging Face key you provide.

Summaries and questions are processed by Ollama, which runs on your PC.

This website

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, and the installers by GitHub. Like any host, GitHub receives visitors' IP addresses to deliver the pages; see the GitHub privacy statement.

To show the latest version and the download count, your browser queries GitHub's public API. The website uses no cookies and no analytics.

After you click “Download”, two optional questions are offered: your country and how you heard about TranscriPilote. If you answer, only those answers and the version number are stored, with the provider Supabase, with no name or e-mail address. They are only used to know where the software is used. Your browser remembers locally that you have already been asked.

Your rights

For any question about your data, or to exercise your rights of access and erasure, write through the Contact page.

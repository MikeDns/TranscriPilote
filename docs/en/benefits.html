Benefits

Why choose TranscriPilote?

Six reasons, one idea: your meetings belong to you, and they stay with you.

  1. Privacy

    Nothing leaves your PC

    Transcription, summaries and questions are processed on your computer. No third-party server sees your conversations.

    • Ideal for customer and HR data
    • No account to create
  2. Cost

    Free, unlimited

    No subscription, no minute quota, no “premium” version. Transcribe as many meetings as you like.

    • No advertising
    • No credit card required
  3. Compatibility

    Ready for the workstation

    No GPU or administrator rights needed: TranscriPilote installs in your Windows profile, even on a locked-down computer.

    • Windows 10 and 11
    • Runs on an ordinary laptop
  4. Availability

    Even without internet

    Once installed, TranscriPilote works offline: on the train, on the move or on a restricted network.

  5. Productivity

    Less note-taking, more listening

    Focus on the conversation: the report, key points and action items are ready after the meeting.

    • Summary and actions in a few minutes
    • Direct questions about the content
  6. Control

    You stay in charge

    Updates are prepared in the background and installed when you decide, with the option to go back to the previous version.

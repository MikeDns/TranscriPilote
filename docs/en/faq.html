FAQ

Frequently asked questions

A question that isn't here? Ask it on the project page.

Is TranscriPilote free?

Yes. TranscriPilote is free, with no subscription, no minute limit and no advertising, for personal and professional use alike.

Are my meetings sent over the internet?

No. Transcription, summaries and questions are processed on your PC. Nothing is sent to an online service, except the backup to your own cloud if you turn it on.

What does this site collect when I download?

The number of downloads is counted by GitHub, which hosts the installer. After you click “Download”, two optional questions are offered: your country and how you heard about TranscriPilote. Only these answers are recorded, with no name or e-mail address, and you can skip them. The app itself never sends anything.

Do I need a GPU or administrator rights?

No. TranscriPilote runs on an ordinary PC and installs in the user's Windows profile, without administrator rights.

Can I transcribe a Teams, Zoom or Meet meeting?

Yes. TranscriPilote records your online meetings, whatever the tool (Teams, Zoom, Meet…), as well as your in-room meetings. It also accepts audio and video files.

Which languages are supported?

French and English, with automatic language detection. The app itself is available in French and English, and summaries are written in the language chosen for the interface.

How long does a transcription take?

Without a GPU, allow between half and twice the length of the recording, depending on the accuracy level chosen. Transcription runs in the background: you can keep using the app.

How do updates work?

TranscriPilote checks at startup whether a new version exists and prepares it in the background. It installs when you click “Restart to update”, without reinstalling or losing your data.

Is there a tutorial to get started?

Yes. At first launch, TranscriPilote offers a guided tour of about one minute, which you can decline. Restart it at any time with the help button at the bottom of the menu, or in Settings, Help section. After an update, only what's new is shown.

How can I contact the author of TranscriPilote?

Through the Contact page of Mike DANSOU's website, for a question, a partnership or feedback. To report a problem in the app, please use the issue page.

Is it spelled “Transcri Pilote” or “Transcrit Pilote”?

The official name is written as one word: TranscriPilote. It is sometimes written “Transcri Pilote” or “Transcrit Pilote”.

Convinced?

TranscriPilote is free and installs in a few minutes.

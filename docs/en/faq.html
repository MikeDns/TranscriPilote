Frequently asked questions

A question that is not here? Ask it on the project page.

Is TranscriPilote free?

Yes. TranscriPilote is free, with no subscription, no minute limit and no ads, for personal and professional use.

Are my meetings sent over the Internet?

No. Transcription, summaries and questions are processed on your PC. Nothing is sent to an online service, except the backup to your own cloud if you turn it on. See the privacy policy.

Who publishes TranscriPilote?

TranscriPilote is published by MikeDns Labs, the name under which Mike DANSOU publishes his software. He designs and maintains it himself. See the legal notice.

Do I need a graphics card or admin rights?

No. TranscriPilote runs on an ordinary PC and installs in the user's Windows profile, with no admin rights.

Can it transcribe a Teams, Zoom or Meet meeting?

Yes. TranscriPilote records your online meetings, whatever the tool, as well as in-room meetings. It also accepts audio and video files.

Which languages are supported?

English and French, with automatic language detection. The app itself is available in English and French, and summaries are written in the interface language.

How long does a transcription take?

Without a graphics card, allow between half and twice the length of the recording, depending on the accuracy level. Transcription runs in the background: you can keep using the app.

I forgot to stop the recording. What can I do?

In the history, the scissors button opens “Trim the meeting”: choose the useful part to the minute, then transcribe only that part or cut the recording. Optional aids in the settings can also stop it when nobody is speaking.

How do updates work?

TranscriPilote checks for a new version at startup and prepares it in the background. It installs when you decide, never during a recording or a transcription, without reinstalling or losing data.

How do I back up my meetings?

In Settings, “Backup copy”, connect your own Google Drive or OneDrive: once, in your browser. Then “Back up now” copies your meetings, transcripts, summaries and audio recordings to a “TranscriPilote” folder in your account. Each computer gets its own subfolder, named after it, so two PCs connected to the same account never erase each other. Backup is optional, and your data goes through no server of ours: it goes from your PC to your account.

Is there a tutorial?

Yes. On first launch, TranscriPilote offers a guided tour of about one minute, which you can decline. Restart it at any time with the help button at the bottom of the menu.

How do I contact the author?

Through the Contact page, for a question, a partnership or feedback. To report a problem in the app, use the issue page instead.

Is it spelled “Transcri Pilote”?

The official name is one word: TranscriPilote. It is sometimes written “Transcri Pilote” or “Transcrit Pilote”.

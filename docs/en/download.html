Built-in updates

New versions are prepared in the background and install in one click, without reinstalling. Your data is kept.

Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”

This is usual for recent software. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”. Each version is published with its SHA-256 checksums (file SHA256SUMS.txt), which let you check that the file has not been modified.

Where is my data stored? How do I uninstall?

Your meetings and settings are in %LOCALAPPDATA%\TranscriPilote. To uninstall: Windows Settings → Apps → TranscriPilote. Your data is kept; delete this folder to erase everything.
